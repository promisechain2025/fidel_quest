Story narration clips (optional; the reader falls back to word-by-word audio).

Amharic:  public/audio/fidel/stories/<story-id>-<page>.mp3
Tigrinya: public/audio/fidel/stories/ti/<story-id>-<page>.mp3

Filenames + sentences are listed in docs/story-recording-script.md
(and docs/story-recording.csv). After adding clips, run:
  npm run gen:audio-manifest
so the app knows to play them.
