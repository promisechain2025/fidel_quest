# 1.4.6 release notes

Version is **1.4.6** in `package.json`, Android `versionName`, and the Xcode `MARKETING_VERSION` (Debug and Release). The Play `versionCode` fallback stays 118 and the iOS `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` stays 10; CI sets both at upload time.

1.4.5 is in store review, so the next upload needs a new marketing version. Paste the notes below into App Store Connect "What's New" and the Play "Release notes".

## What changed since 1.4.5 (PR #115)
- Wudase Mariam for Wednesday: ዳግሚት and ዲበ ምድር are now separate units; ኆኅተ ምሥራቅ, ወኃይለ ልዑል, እምኅፅነ አቡሁ and ኆኀተ ምሥራቅ are joined; ኲሎሙ and እምሱራፌል are corrected; the duplicate ሰብእ is removed; the chant marks are corrected.
- Wudase Mariam for Thursday: ነሥአ, ወለሐኰ and ኄር are corrected; ብእሲት, እምዕፅ, እምቅድመ ዓለም and ብዕለ ጥበቡ are now ተጣይ.
- አማን is ተጣይ on every day.
- A word ending in a ሳድስ letter is never ወዳቂ except after ዝ. ይመጽእ is ሰያፍ, and the rest, including ትንሣኤ ምውታን in the Creed, are ተጣይ.
