# 1.4.5 release notes (DRAFT, not submitted to any store)

Version is **1.4.5** in `package.json`, Android `versionName`, and the Xcode `MARKETING_VERSION` (Debug and Release). The Play `versionCode` fallback stays 118 and the iOS `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` stays 10; CI sets both at upload time.

iOS 1.4.4 shipped on 6 November 2026 and that train is closed, so a new App Store build needs this marketing version. Paste into App Store Connect "What's New" and the Play "Release notes" by hand once Yosief approves.

## What changed since the released 1.4.4 (PRs #112 and #113)
- #112 Ge'ez spelling in the Lord's Prayer and the Creed (joined split words; ውስተ, ውላጤ, ውልድ). Monday's መለኮት chant mark is tetay.
- #113 Tetay on እግዚአብሔር and ዘእግዚአብሔር, the Tuesday line, and the remaining prayer spellings. Wudase Mariam, all seven days.
